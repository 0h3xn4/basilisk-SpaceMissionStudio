Qt and PySide6 in this package
==============================

SpaceMissionStudio uses the Qt toolkit (Qt 6.11.2, including Qt WebEngine,
which contains Chromium) through PySide6 (Qt for Python) 6.11.2. These
libraries are used under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3
(LGPL-3.0-only). Their copyright belongs to The Qt Company Ltd and the
other authors named in the Qt source code.

* The text of the LGPL-3.0, with the GNU General Public License version 3 that
  it refers to, is in licenses/LGPL-3.0-only.txt.
* The libraries are separate shared files (the .so files, or .dll files on
  Windows, and the PySide6 extension modules) in the folder
  lib/python3.14/site-packages/PySide6 and the folders next to it. They are
  not changed, except that the Qt modules SpaceMissionStudio does not use and
  that Qt offers only under the GPL are left out. You may replace the
  libraries with your own build of Qt and PySide6 of a compatible version.
* The complete corresponding source code is attached to every
  SpaceMissionStudio release on GitHub as qt-everywhere-src-6.11.2.tar.xz and
  pyside-setup-everywhere-src-6.11.2.tar.xz, with their SHA-256 in
  SHA256SUMS-sources: https://github.com/0h3xn4/basilisk_space-mission-studio/releases
  The Qt Company publishes the same archives at https://download.qt.io/ .
  The licences of the third-party code inside Qt and Qt WebEngine are listed
  at https://doc.qt.io/qt-6.11/licenses-used-in-qt.html and
  https://doc.qt.io/qt-6.11/qtwebengine-licensing.html, and are in the source
  archives.
