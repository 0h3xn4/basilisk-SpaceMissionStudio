# Wording drafts for the owner's approval

**Status: DRAFTS. None of this text is used anywhere yet.** Not legal
advice. The brief (`docs/LEGAL_AND_WEBSITE.md` §2.3) asks for two short
sentences, one on non-affiliation and one on no warranty, for the owner
to approve. It forbids legal text written by Claude. The drafts
therefore state facts and point to the licence's own articles. They do
not restate or extend the EUPL's terms. The website was cancelled (Q7),
so the drafts are proposed for the places below.

Approve each draft as it is, change it, or reject it. Placements marked
**app change** or **output change** also need an explicit yes, because
they change what the app shows or writes.

## W1 Non-affiliation

> SpaceMissionStudio is an independent personal project. It is not
> affiliated with or endorsed by the organisations named in its
> documentation, such as NASA, ESA, the AVS Lab of the University of
> Colorado Boulder, CNES or The Qt Company.

Proposed places: the README (root fork section and
`SpaceMissionStudio/README.md`); the user manual's introduction. These
are documentation changes only.

The sentence already exists for CNES in
`docs/reference/data-and-standards.md` and `docs/user-manual/idm-cic.md`.

## W2 No warranty

> SpaceMissionStudio is free of charge and comes without warranty; see
> Articles 7 and 8 of the EUPL-1.2. Check its results independently
> before you use them for a real mission.

Proposed places:

- the README and the user manual: documentation changes only;
- the About box, beside the licence line (`spacemissionstudio/legal.py`,
  `about_html`): **app change** (one line of text; the About test is
  extended).

Not proposed: a confirmation at first start. It would change a workflow
that users rely on, and the licence applies without it.

## W3 Generated flight software

> This project was generated by SpaceMissionStudio for
> software-in-the-loop tests. It is not qualified for flight. Review and
> test it under your own process before any operational use.

Proposed place: the README that the flight-software export writes into
the generated project. **Output change:** the generated files and their
golden copies change, and the export's notice (decision P1: keep it as
it is) is not affected.

## What does not change

- The EUPL text and the licence headers.
- The ISC notice in the generated C files (P1, decided: leave).
- "Not a certification" in the README, which already states a fact.
