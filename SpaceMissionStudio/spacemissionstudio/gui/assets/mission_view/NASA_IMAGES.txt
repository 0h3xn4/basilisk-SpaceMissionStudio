NASA images in this folder
==========================

earth_day_4k.jpg, earth_day_2k.jpg
  From "Blue Marble - A Seamless Image Mosaic of the Earth", NASA Goddard
  Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, visualization 2915
  (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915), files bluemarble-west-4096.png and
  bluemarble-east-4096.png, joined and resized.
  Credit: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
  The Blue Marble data is courtesy of Reto Stockli (NASA/GSFC).

stars_4k.jpg, stars_2k.jpg
  From "Deep Star Maps 2020", NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific
  Visualization Studio, visualization 4851 (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851),
  file starmap_2020_4k.exr, tone-mapped to sRGB.
  Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
  Star data: Hipparcos and Tycho-2 (ESA), UCAC3 (U.S. Naval Observatory),
  Gaia DR2 (ESA/Gaia/DPAC).
